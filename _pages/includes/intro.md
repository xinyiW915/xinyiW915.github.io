I am a Research Associate in the <a class="noul" href="https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/rainbow/">Graphics and Interaction (Rainbow) Group</a> at the <a class="noul" href="https://www.cam.ac.uk/">University of Cambridge</a>, under the supervision of <a class="noul" href="https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~rkm38/">Professor Rafał Mantiuk</a>. I work on quality metrics for HDR game streaming in collaboration with Sony Interactive Entertainment.

Before that, I was a PhD student in the <a class="noul" href="https://vilab.blogs.bristol.ac.uk/">Visual Information (VI) Laboratory</a> at the <a class="noul" href="https://www.bristol.ac.uk/">University of Bristol</a>, supervised by <a class="noul" href="https://angkats.github.io/">Dr. Angeliki Katsenou</a>, <a class="noul" href="https://shawnshenjx.github.io/">Dr. Shawn Shen</a> and <a class="noul" href="https://david-bull.github.io/">Prof. David Bull</a>, and funded by the <a class="noul" href="https://www.myworld-creates.com/">UKRI MyWorld Strength in Places Programme</a> Scholarship.

My main research interests include video quality assessment (VQA), computer vision, and computer graphics. I am particularly interested in perceptual quality, content delivery, and VQA algorithms for both SDR and HDR content.
